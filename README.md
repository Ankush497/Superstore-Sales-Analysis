# 🛒 Superstore Sales Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing **Superstore sales performance** using Microsoft Power BI.

The objective of this project is to transform raw business data into meaningful insights by analysing key performance indicators such as **Sales, Quantity, Profit, Returns, Customer Segments, Product Categories, Regions, Shipping Modes, and Monthly Performance**.

An interactive Power BI dashboard was created to provide a clear overview of business performance and help identify important sales and profit trends across different business dimensions.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyse overall sales performance.
- Monitor total profit and quantity sold.
- Track product returns.
- Identify the best-performing customer segments.
- Analyse sales across different product categories.
- Compare business performance across regions.
- Analyse profit generated by different shipping modes.
- Identify monthly sales and profit trends.
- Build an interactive and easy-to-understand Power BI dashboard.

# 📈 Dashboard Analysis

## 👥 Sales by Customer Segment

The dashboard analyses sales performance across different customer segments:

- **Consumer**
- **Corporate**
- **Home Office**

The Consumer segment generates the highest sales, making it an important contributor to overall business performance.

---

## 🛍️ Sales by Category

Sales performance is analysed across three major product categories:

- **Office Supplies**
- **Technology**
- **Furniture**

This analysis helps identify which product categories contribute the most to total sales.

---

## 🌍 Sales by Region

The dashboard compares sales performance across four regions:

- **Central**
- **East**
- **South**
- **West**

Regional analysis helps identify high-performing markets and areas that may require additional business attention.

---

## 🚚 Profit by Shipping Mode

Profitability is analysed across different shipping methods:

- **Standard Class**
- **Second Class**
- **First Class**
- **Same Day**

This helps understand how shipping options contribute to overall profitability.

---

## 📅 Sales and Profit by Month

The dashboard includes a monthly trend analysis comparing:

📊 **Sales**  
📈 **Profit**

This visualization helps identify:

- Monthly performance trends.
- High-performing periods.
- Changes in sales and profitability.
- Potential seasonal patterns.

# 💡 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis:

### 🥇 Customer Segment

The **Consumer segment** generates the highest sales compared to Corporate and Home Office customers.

### 🛍️ Product Category

**Office Supplies** is the highest-performing category in terms of sales.

### 🌎 Regional Performance

The **West region** contributes the highest sales among the analysed regions.

### 🚚 Shipping Performance

**Standard Class** generates the highest profit among the available shipping modes.

### 📈 Monthly Trends

Sales and profit vary throughout the year, demonstrating the importance of analysing monthly performance instead of relying only on overall totals.
